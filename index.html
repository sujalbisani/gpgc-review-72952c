Where the numbers come from. Tonnage is invoice lines from the FY sheet of Year 2026.xlsx (April to August) and the September MRM file. The customer is the real buyer, so the five channel companies that invoice on our behalf are netted out rather than counted as customers.

Money. Outstanding, collections and advances are the Debtors Outstanding and Customers Collection files circulated daily. Age is counted from the invoice date to the as-of date, because the channels set their own due dates differently and that is the only basis on which they compare.
